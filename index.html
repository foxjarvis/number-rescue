Door 01

Get your old number back

If you dropped the number recently, this is almost always faster than fighting with every app one by one. Every code goes right back to finding you.

📞
Still your carrier, just cancelled?Call and ask to reactivate it
1

Call your old carrier

Most carriers hold a cancelled number for about 30-90 days before it's reassigned. Ask directly: "Can I reactivate my old number on a new line or a cheap plan?"
2

If yes — get it on any SIM

Even the cheapest prepaid plan works. You just need it live long enough to receive texts.
3

Let the old codes roll in

Log into your stuck accounts, request a new code, and it lands on this reactivated line.
4

Then update every account to your real number

While you have it, go update your number everywhere before you let it go again.
🔁
Switched carriers / ported away?Port it back or forward it
1

Ask the new carrier if it's still assigned to you

If you ported your number to a new carrier (not cancelled it), it's just alive on a different SIM — pop that SIM back in your phone, or forward its texts.
2

No SIM to swap? Forward the texts

Some carriers let you forward SMS to another number or an app from their account settings online.
3

Fully gone? Try a number-recovery request

Call the carrier that has it now, explain it was recently yours, and ask about a "number release" — it's not guaranteed, but it costs nothing to ask.

Door 03

Lock it down for good

Do this once per account you care about and a lost number can never lock you out again.

✓

Switch from text codes to an authenticator app

Google Authenticator, Authy, or your password manager. The codes live on your phone itself, not your number.
✓

Save your backup codes

Most apps hand you a one-time list when you turn on 2FA. Screenshot it. One of those gets you in with no phone at all.
✓

Add a recovery email

A second way in that has nothing to do with your number.
✓

Update your number before you cancel the old line

While the old phone still works, log into everything and change the number on file. Thirty minutes now beats weeks of this later.
Fastest win: if you take nothing else from this page, turn on an authenticator app for your email and your bank. Those two unlock everything else if something goes wrong.
